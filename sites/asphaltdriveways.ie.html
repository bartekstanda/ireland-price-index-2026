3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Asphalt Driveways Ireland)

How much does an asphalt driveway cost in Ireland?

Resurfacing a sound existing tarmac or concrete driveway with a 40mm SMA asphalt overlay costs €55 to €80 per m² (€2,800 to €4,200 for a typical 50 m² 2-car driveway). A full dig-out with a 150–200mm compacted Clause 804 stone sub-base, 60mm binder course and 40mm SMA surface course costs €75 to €115 per m² (€3,800 to €5,800 for 50 m², or €6,000 to €9,000 for an 80 m² 3–4 car driveway).

What is the difference between SMA asphalt and tarmacadam?

Traditional tarmacadam uses graded stone in a softer binder that can scuff when modern heavy cars or EVs turn their power steering on the spot. Stone Mastic Asphalt (SMA) uses a gap-graded stone skeleton bound with fibre-modified bitumen — the stones lock directly together, giving a denser, smoother, scuff-resistant surface that lasts 20 to 25 years.

Can you lay new asphalt over an existing concrete or tarmac driveway?

Yes, provided the existing slab or tarmac is structurally sound, does not rock or hold deep puddles, and the threshold height at your house damp-proof course (DPC) and road footpath allows an extra 40mm depth. An overlay costs €55 to €80 per m² after sweeping, joint-sealing and spraying a hot K1-40 bitumen tack coat.

Do I need planning permission to tarmac or asphalt my front garden?

Under Class 6 of the Planning and Development Regulations, paving or surfacing a front garden up to 5 m² is automatically exempt, and areas over 5 m² are exempt provided surface water is drained within the curtilage of your property (for example into a lawn border, gravel soakaway strip or ACO linear channel connected to a soakaway) rather than flowing out onto the public footpath. Widening the vehicular entrance pier opening onto a public road always requires council planning permission.

How soon can I park my car on a new asphalt driveway?

You can walk on a new asphalt driveway within a few hours once the roller finishes, but wait 48 to 72 hours (and up to 5 days during warm summer weather) before parking cars on it. For the first month, avoid turning the steering wheel while the car is stationary, and do not place motorbike kickstands or caravan jockey wheels directly on fresh asphalt without a spreader board.

Is resin bound or SMA asphalt better for an Irish driveway?

SMA asphalt is tougher under heavy axle loads, farm machinery and turning vans, and costs less on medium-to-large driveways. If you prefer a decorative natural stone finish that is fully SuDS permeable without separate channel drains, compare prices on our sister guide at resinbounddriveways.ie.
