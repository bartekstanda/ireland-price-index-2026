3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Attic Insulation Grant)

How much is the SEAI attic insulation grant?

€2,000 for a detached house, €1,500 for a semi-D or end of terrace, €1,400 for a mid-terrace and €1,100 for an apartment. The higher rate, for certain welfare recipients, HAP and RAS landlords and first-time buyers, is €2,500, €1,900, €1,800 and €1,400.

What does attic insulation cost after the grant?

Typically €300 to €600 for a semi-D, €400 to €700 for a detached house, €200 to €800 for a mid-terrace and €100 to €400 for an apartment, after the standard grant. See the cost guide .

How long does it take?

Most attics are done in a day. The wait is SEAI's approval, which has to come before anything is bought or started. A floored attic, or one full of storage, takes longer.

Is there much mess?

Very little in the rooms below: the work happens in the attic, through the hatch. Clear a path to the hatch, and anything stored up there may need to come down first.

What guarantee do I get?

Ask for it in writing: the depth and U-value reached, the walkway, the hatch and the lagging, and what the contractor stands over afterwards. The contractor fills in SEAI's paperwork for your claim.

Who qualifies for the attic insulation grant?

Homeowners and landlords whose home was built and lived in before 2011 and has an MPRN. The work must be done by a contractor on SEAI's register, and SEAI must approve the grant before you buy materials or start.
