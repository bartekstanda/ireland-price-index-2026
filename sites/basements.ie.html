3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Basements.ie)

How much does a basement conversion cost in Ireland?

€1,000 to €2,500 a m² to turn an existing basement or cellar into a finished room, so €30,000 to €100,000 for most whole conversions. Waterproofing on its own is €50 to €180 a m². Digging a new basement is €3,000 to €6,000 a m². See the cost guide .

How long does it take?

6 to 12 weeks for most conversions. The waterproofing itself is 1 to 2 weeks, then 2 to 3 weeks of finishing. Digging out takes longer, because the underpinning goes in stages. See how long it takes .

Is there a grant for a basement conversion?

No. There's no national grant for converting or waterproofing a basement. Radon work isn't grant-aided either, but the EPA tests the house again for free after the work.

Will it make a mess of the house?

Some. Old plaster and floors come out first, so expect dust and a skip. Most waterproofing work stays inside the basement. Digging out means spoil leaving through a side passage or the house, so the company plans the route before they start.

What guarantee do I get?

Ask for the waterproofing guarantee in writing: who gives it, for how long and what it covers. A sump pump needs a yearly service to keep working, and often to keep the guarantee.

Do I need planning permission?

Not usually to convert an existing basement inside the house. A new basement dug under the house or garden, a light well at the front or a protected structure is a question for the council. See planning permission .
