3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Bathroom Renovations Limerick)

How much mess is there?

The room is stripped to the walls, so there's dust and a skip outside for a few days. A good company sheets the stairs and landing, cuts tiles outside where it can and tidies up every evening.

Is the work guaranteed?

Ask for it in writing: a guarantee on the workmanship, the makers' guarantees on the shower, the valve and the suite, and the guarantee on the tanking. The electrician gives you a completion certificate.

Who comes out to price it?

One bathroom renovation company that covers your part of Limerick. They see the room, check the floor, the hot water and the waste, then price it in writing.

Do I need to apply for a council grant before bathroom work starts in Limerick?

Yes. Limerick City and County Council requires written grant approval before any adaptation work starts on site. Work started before approval is not covered.

What is the Housing Adaptation Grant cap?

Up to €40,000 for adapting a home for a person with a disability, or up to €8,000 under the Mobility Aids Grant for household incomes up to €37,500.

Why is keeping the existing bathroom layout cheaper?

Keeping the toilet, basin and shower in the same positions avoids re-routing the 110 mm soil stack and floor waste pipes through joists or concrete slabs.
