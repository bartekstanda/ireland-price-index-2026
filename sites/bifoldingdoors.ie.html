3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Bifolding Doors Ireland)

How much do bifold doors cost in Ireland supplied and fitted?

In thermally broken aluminium, a 3-panel bifold door (2.4m–3.0m) costs €3,600 to €5,800 supplied and fitted, a 4-panel set (3.2m–4.0m) is €4,800 to €7,600, a 5-panel set (4.2m–5.0m) is €6,000 to €9,400, and a 6-panel set (5.2m–6.0m) is €7,200 to €11,500. Timber alu-clad frames run from €4,800 to €7,400 for 3 panels up to €9,600 to €14,200 for 6 panels.

How much does it cost to knock through a back wall for bifold doors?

Widening an existing rear window or standard 1.8m patio door opening—including propping the masonry, installing an RSJ structural steel lintel on concrete padstones, removing the masonry below, and plastering the reveals—typically adds €1,800 to €3,500.

Are bifold doors draughty or leaky in Irish winter weather?

Modern bottom-rolling aluminium and alu-clad bifold doors tested to BS 6375 / EN 12208 achieve Class 4 air permeability and 600 Pa water tightness when fitted with a weathered threshold or a recessed flush track paired with an external ACO slot drain.

What is a traffic door on a bifold set?

A traffic door (or lead door) is a single hinged panel built into the bifold run with its own lever handle and multi-point lock. It lets you nip out to the garden on a wet Tuesday morning like a normal back door without folding back the whole stack.

Should I pick an odd or even number of bifold panels?

An odd number of panels folding in one direction (such as 3+0 or 5+0) or an even number split into a 3+1 configuration gives you a hinged traffic door. Four panels folding all one way (4+0) cannot have a swinging traffic door on the lead end because the end leaf pivots directly off the jamb.

What VAT rate applies to bifold doors in Ireland?

When bifold doors are supplied and fitted together by the installation company on a residential home, the entire package qualifies for Ireland's reduced 13.5% VAT rate (subject to Revenue's two-thirds rule), rather than 23% supply-only VAT.
