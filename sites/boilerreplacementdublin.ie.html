3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Boiler Replacement Dublin)

How much is a new boiler in Dublin?

It depends on the boiler you have now, the size you need and whether the boiler or the flue moves. Every job is in our price guide , and the installer gives you a fixed price after a look at the boiler.

How long does it take to replace a boiler?

One day for a like-for-like swap in the same spot. Two days if the boiler moves or it's a back boiler or old system to a combi.

Is the installer registered?

Gas work only goes to a Registered Gas Installer (RGI). Ask to see the RGI ID card. You get a certificate after the work, a CERT 2 for a boiler swap.

Is there a grant for a new boiler?

No. SEAI has no grant for a new gas or oil boiler. Its €700 heating controls grant can apply in a home built and occupied before 2011.

Can I still replace a gas boiler?

Yes. No rule stops a like-for-like replacement in an existing home. It must be a condensing boiler where practicable.

Is the work guaranteed?

A new boiler carries the maker's warranty, usually on condition of a yearly service. Ask the installer about their guarantee on the fitting.
