3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Boiler Service Dublin)

How much is a boiler service in Dublin?

A gas boiler service in Dublin is €80–€199, and a typical price is about €120. An oil boiler service is €80–€220. A repair call-out is €80–€199, with parts extra.

How long does a boiler service take?

About an hour for most gas boilers, longer for an oil boiler or one that hasn't been serviced in years.

Is the engineer registered?

Gas work only goes to a Registered Gas Installer (RGI). It's the law in Ireland. Ask to see the RGI ID card: it has a photo and a registration number. Oil boilers go to an oil technician.

How fast can someone come?

The engineer rings you, usually within one working day, and agrees a time. Breakdowns are seen sooner than routine services.

Do I get a certificate?

Yes. After gas work the RGI gives you a certificate, the Declaration of Conformance. Keep it with the boiler's papers.

Is the work guaranteed?

Ask the engineer about their guarantee on the work and on any parts before you agree the price. New parts carry the maker's warranty.
