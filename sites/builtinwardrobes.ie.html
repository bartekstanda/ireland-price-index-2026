3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Built In Wardrobes)

How much do built-in wardrobes cost in Ireland?

A small fitted wardrobe of about 1.5 m costs €800 to €1,706. A 2.4 m double-bedroom wardrobe with hinged doors is €2,236 to €4,000, sliding doors on the same wall €2,500 to €4,588, and bespoke built-ins €4,100 to €8,600. Our full price guide .

Is there a fitted wardrobe company near me?

Yes, in all 26 counties. We pass your request to one fitted wardrobe company that covers your county, and they come out to measure the room. Wardrobes by county .

Sliding or hinged doors?

Sliding doors need no room to open, so they suit narrow bedrooms and rooms where the bed is close to the wall. Hinged doors open the whole wardrobe at once and cost less on the same wall. Sliding or hinged .

How long does a fitted wardrobe take?

Three to six weeks from the approved drawing to fitting day for most companies. The fitting itself is a couple of hours for a small wardrobe and a day for a big one.

Can you fit a wardrobe under a sloped ceiling?

Yes. It's cut to the slope on site, with drawers or short hanging along the low wall and long hanging where the ceiling is highest. Attic wardrobes .

Will they take away my old wardrobe?

Ask for it on the quote. Taking out and taking away an old wardrobe usually costs €150 to €300.
