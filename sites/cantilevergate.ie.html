3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Cantilever Gates Ireland)

How much does a cantilever sliding gate cost in Ireland?

In Ireland, a manual steel cantilever sliding gate costs €2,800 to €4,200 supplied and fitted, while a residential electric steel cantilever gate costs €3,800 to €6,800. An electric powder-coated aluminium cantilever gate costs €4,800 to €8,500, and wide commercial cantilever gates (5m to 9m opening) cost €5,500 to €12,000. Pouring a new reinforced concrete counterbalance pad behind the pier adds €600 to €2,000.

What is a cantilever gate and how does it stay up without a ground track?

A cantilever sliding gate has no wheels touching the driveway surface and no steel rail crossing the entrance. Instead, two heavy-duty 8-wheel roller carriages are bolted to a concrete pad behind one pier, and an enclosed steel or aluminium monorail channel welded to the bottom of the gate slides over those fixed rollers. A 33% to 40% counterbalance tail behind the pier balances the weight of the gate leaf as it cantilevers out across the opening.

How much side run-back space do I need for a cantilever gate?

Because the gate frame includes the counterbalance tail that stays spanned between the two roller carriages when the gate is closed, total gate length — and the straight run-back space needed behind one pier — is roughly 1.35 to 1.4 times the clear entrance width. For example, a 3.6m entrance needs about 4.9m of clear run-back; a 4.0m entrance needs about 5.4m.

Why choose a cantilever gate instead of a tracked sliding gate?

Choose a cantilever gate whenever your driveway is surfaced in loose gravel, stone chippings or tar-and-chip (which jam a ground V-rail), where the entrance has a drainage crown or uneven cross-fall, where you already have finished cobblelock or resin that you do not want to saw-cut across the opening, or on commercial yards where heavy lorries would crush a surface rail.

Do I need planning permission for a cantilever driveway gate in Ireland?

At an existing domestic entrance, installing a cantilever sliding gate up to 2.0 metres high is exempt from planning permission under Class 5 of Schedule 2, Part 1 of the Planning and Development Regulations 2001 (S.I. No. 600 of 2001), provided you do not widen the roadside entrance or obstruct traffic sightlines.

What safety features are required on an electric cantilever gate?

Under HSA guidance and I.S. EN 12453, an electric cantilever gate must include motor force limitation with encoder slowdown, vertical resistive safety edges on the closing nose and rear guide-post shearing zone, roller-carriage finger guards, and inner and outer infrared photocells.
