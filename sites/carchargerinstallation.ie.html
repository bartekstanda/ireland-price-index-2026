3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Car Charger Installation)

How much does it cost to install a car charger at home?

A 7 kW smart car charger costs €1,000 to €1,500 fitted in Ireland, including VAT, with a standard fit of up to 5 metres of cable. The SEAI grant takes €300 off, so a standard fit comes to €700 to €1,200. A longer cable run, a trench to a garage or post, and work on an older fuse board come on top.

How much is the SEAI home charger grant?

Up to €300, covering 100% of the charger and installation cost up to that amount.

Who can get the grant?

Homeowners with off-street parking at the home, whether or not they own an electric car yet. One charger per MPRN, and not a property that already had a home charger grant.

Can I start before the grant is approved?

No. Any work before the start date on the Letter of Offer, and any spending before approval, is ineligible.

Which charger suits my car?

A 7 kW smart charger with a Type 2 connector suits a fully electric car or a plug-in hybrid. Type 2 is the EU standard for AC charging and the plug the SEAI grant is for, and 7 kW is the fastest a normal single-phase home supply allows. A car with an older Type 1 socket uses the same charger with a Type 2 to Type 1 cable, or a tethered Type 1 charger.

Tethered or untethered?

Tethered has the cable fixed to the charger and costs €100 to €200 more; untethered has a Type 2 socket for your own cable and suits any car.
