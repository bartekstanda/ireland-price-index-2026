3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Cavity Wall Insulation)

How long does it take?

Usually one day for a house. The contractor drills a pattern of small holes in the outer wall, pumps the insulation in and fills the holes to match.

Is it messy?

Most of the work is outside. Someone may need to look at vents inside. The drilled holes are filled to match the wall.

Do I need planning permission?

No. SEAI says planning permission is not required for internal or cavity insulation.

Can every house have cavity wall insulation?

No. Solid walls and timber frame walls can't be filled, and a wall with damp problems needs fixing first. See is my house suitable?

I already had a wall grant. Can I get another?

You may apply for a second wall insulation measure. After a cavity grant: internal or external insulation. After internal: external only. After external: none. A second cavity grant isn't available.

Will it make the house warmer?

Usually, if the cavity was empty. Less heat leaks out through the walls, so rooms hold their heat longer and the heating runs less. How much you notice depends on the house, the heating and how warm you keep it.
