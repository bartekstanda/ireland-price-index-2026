3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Cavity Wall Insulation Grants)

How much is the cavity wall insulation grant?

SEAI pays a fixed cavity wall insulation grant: €1,800 for a detached house, €1,300 for a semi-detached or end-of-terrace house, €850 for a mid-terrace house, €700 for an apartment. Homeowners on certain welfare payments, and landlords with a HAP or RAS tenant, get the higher rate: €2,300, €1,700, €1,100 and €900.

Who qualifies?

The home must have an MPRN and have been built and first lived in before 2011, with no earlier SEAI wall grant on that MPRN. You must own it (owner-occupiers, landlords, holiday home owners and management companies can apply), every outside wall must be insulated, the installer must be SEAI-registered for cavity wall insulation, and you must have grant approval before any work starts.

What does it cost after the grant?

Cavity wall insulation typically costs €2,350 for a detached house, €1,585 for a semi-detached or end of terrace, €1,250 for a mid-terrace and €1,036 for an apartment. After the SEAI grant about €550, €285, €400 and €336 are left to pay. On the higher rate, a semi-detached house's grant covers the typical cost.

Can work start before approval?

Work started before your grant is approved doesn't qualify, and neither do materials bought before it. SEAI pays nothing for that work, and there's no applying afterwards for the same job. Apply, get the offer, accept it, then book the installer.

How do I apply?

Online, with your MPRN and the installer's name and SEAI ID. Usually approved in minutes. Accept within 30 days, finish and claim within 8 months.

When is the grant paid?

Usually 4 to 6 weeks after SEAI has the signed Declaration of Works and the Request for Payment form.
