3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Circumcision.ie)

Who does the circumcision?

One Medical Council registered doctor or clinic. If you ask, we pass your enquiry to them, and they examine you, or your son, and give you the price.

Is circumcision free on the HSE?

With a medical reason, yes, as a public patient after a GP referral: there's no hospital charge, but there's a waiting list. Without a medical reason there's no national HSE policy, and each health region decides.

Does health insurance cover circumcision?

When it's medically necessary, in a hospital or clinic your plan covers. Not for personal, religious or cultural reasons. Waiting periods apply if you're new to health insurance.

How much does circumcision cost privately in Ireland?

Under local anaesthetic in a clinic: €300 to €600 for a baby, €700 to €1,500 for a boy of 1 to 15, and €750 to €1,900 for a man. Our price guide .

Can I claim tax relief on circumcision?

With a medical reason, yes: 20% of what you pay yourself, about €150 to €380 back on an adult circumcision. Without one, ask Revenue first.

Who contacts me if I send my details?

One Medical Council registered doctor or clinic, not a list. They ring you to arrange a consultation, and nothing is booked until you agree.
