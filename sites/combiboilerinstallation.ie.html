3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Combi Boiler Installation)

How much is a combi boiler fitted?

A gas combi fitted is €1,800–€4,000, typically about €2,700. An oil combi is €3,500–€5,890. Converting from a cylinder and tanks is €3,400–€4,800.

How long does a combi installation take?

One day for a combi swap. Two for a conversion from a cylinder, or if the boiler moves.

Is the installer registered?

Gas work only goes to a Registered Gas Installer (RGI). Oil combis go to an oil technician. You get a certificate after gas work.

Is there a grant for a combi?

No. SEAI has no grant for a new gas or oil boiler. Its €700 heating controls grant can apply in a home built and occupied before 2011.

Can a combi run two showers?

Not well at the same time. Homes with two or more bathrooms in use together often do better with a system boiler.

Is the work guaranteed?

A new boiler carries the maker's warranty, usually on condition of a yearly service. Ask about the installer's guarantee on the fitting.
