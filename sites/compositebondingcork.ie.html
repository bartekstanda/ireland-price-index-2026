3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Composite Bonding Cork)

How much does composite bonding cost per tooth in Cork?

Composite bonding in Cork typically costs €150 to €350 per tooth. Edge bonding on the biting edge alone is €150 to €180 per tooth, while a full composite veneer covering the front surface is €250 to €350 per tooth.

How much is composite bonding for 6 front teeth in Cork?

Treating the six upper front teeth typically comes to €900 to €2,100 in Cork, and eight teeth come to €1,200 to €2,800.

What is the difference between edge bonding and a composite veneer?

Edge bonding adds tooth-coloured resin only to the biting edge or corners of the tooth to fix chips or wear. A full composite veneer layers resin across the entire visible front surface to change both colour and shape.

Does composite bonding damage your natural tooth enamel?

In most cases no drilling of healthy tooth structure is needed. The surface is cleaned and lightly conditioned with a mild gel so the resin bonds microscopically to your enamel.

How long does composite bonding last before needing a polish or repair?

Composite bonding typically lasts 5 to 7 years with good home care and a routine six-monthly hygienist polish. Edge repairs if a corner chips take one short appointment.

Should I whiten my teeth before getting composite bonding?

Yes. Whitening gels lighten natural tooth enamel but do not change the colour of composite resin. Always complete your dentist-supervised whitening first and wait 10 to 14 days before shade-matching the bonding.
