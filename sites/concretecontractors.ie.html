3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Concrete Contractors)

How much does a concrete slab cost per m² in Ireland?

A slab is priced per m² and a foundation per metre, for your job: the area and depth, the concrete grade, the steel, access for the truck and what's under the slab. Send the job and you get a written price.

How much is a sheet of A393 mesh?

A 4.8 × 2.4 m sheet is €79 to €104, depending on VAT.

How much is a bag of cement?

A 25 kg bag of cement is about €9.65.

What VAT is charged on concrete?

Revenue charges ready-to-pour concrete and concrete blocks at the reduced rate; other building materials are at the standard rate.

How thick should a concrete floor slab be?

At least 150 mm for a ground-supported floor, on at least 200 mm of gas-permeable hardcore (TGD C, 2023).

How wide should a strip foundation be?

400 to 650 mm on good ground, depending on the wall load (TGD A Table 5), and never less than the wall plus 75 mm each side.
