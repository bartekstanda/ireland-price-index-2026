3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Dental Bonding)

What is dental bonding?

Tooth-coloured composite resin added to a tooth and shaped to repair a chip, close a small gap, rebuild a worn edge or cover a stain. Also called composite bonding or tooth bonding.

How much does dental bonding cost in Ireland?

Dental bonding costs €150 to €350 a tooth in Ireland. A small chip or a worn edge is €150 to €300 a tooth. Covering the whole front of a tooth, a composite veneer, is €250 to €350, so six front teeth come to €1,500 to €2,100. Dental treatment is exempt from VAT.

How long does it last?

Five to seven years is usual before bonding needs a polish, a repair or replacing. Some lasts ten years with care. It stains and chips more easily than enamel, so what you eat and drink, and whether you grind your teeth, decide where you land.

Does it hurt?

Usually not. Most bonding needs no injection and no drilling: the dentist cleans the tooth, gently etches the enamel so the composite holds, builds it up and polishes it. If there's decay to remove first, the dentist numbs the tooth.

How many visits does it take?

A consultation, then usually one bonding visit of 30 to 60 minutes a tooth. Several teeth can take one long visit or two shorter ones; whitening first adds about two weeks.

Is bonding permanent?

No. It lasts five to seven years before it needs a polish, a repair or replacing, and because nothing is filed off, it can be removed.
