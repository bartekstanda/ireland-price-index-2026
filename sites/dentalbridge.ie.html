3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Dental Bridges Ireland)

How much does a dental bridge cost in Ireland?

A traditional 3-unit fixed bridge (replacing one missing tooth, anchored on the two teeth either side) costs €1,800 to €3,300 at the clinic — or €1,440 to €2,640 after claiming 20% Form Med 2 tax relief. A minimally invasive Maryland (resin-bonded winged) bridge for a front tooth costs €850 to €1,600 (€680 to €1,280 after 20% relief), and a 4-unit bridge replacing two missing teeth costs €2,400 to €4,400 (€1,920 to €3,520 after 20% relief).

Can I claim 20% tax relief on a dental bridge in Ireland?

Yes. Revenue's Form Med 2 explicitly lists all bridgework — both enamel-retained (Maryland) bridges and tooth-supported or implant-supported fixed bridges — as qualifying non-routine dental treatment. Your dental clinic signs Form Med 2 when you pay, and you claim 20% back through Revenue myAccount.

What is the difference between a traditional bridge and a Maryland bridge?

A traditional 3-unit bridge (€1,800 to €3,300) shapes the two teeth on either side of the gap for crowns and fuses a false tooth (pontic) between them. A Maryland bridge (€850 to €1,600) uses a thin metal or zirconia 'wing' bonded with high-strength resin to the hidden back surface of an adjacent front tooth — leaving the front of your neighbouring tooth untouched.

Is a dental bridge better than a dental implant?

Both cost a similar amount for a single missing tooth (€1,800 to €3,300 for a 3-unit bridge vs €2,000 to €2,800 for a single implant and crown). A dental bridge is much faster (finished in 2 to 3 weeks without surgery or bone grafting) and is ideal when the neighbouring teeth already have large fillings or need crowns anyway. A dental implant is usually preferred when the teeth either side of the gap are completely untouched and healthy.

Does the Medical Card or PRSI cover a dental bridge?

No. The HSE Medical Card (DTSS) covers removable acrylic partial dentures for missing teeth, not fixed bridges or crowns. PRSI Treatment Benefit covers your annual dental exam and €50 towards a scale and polish. Private dental insurance policies with restorative cover often pay 50% to 70% towards a bridge up to an annual cap.

How long does a dental bridge last?

A well-fitted zirconia or porcelain-fused-to-metal fixed bridge typically lasts 10 to 15+ years with daily cleaning under the false tooth (using Superfloss or interdental brushes) and regular dental check-ups.
