3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Emergency Plumbing Sligo)

Who answers when I call 085 708 8979 for an emergency in Sligo?

You speak directly to Arek Olczak of SOS Property Services Sligo, based in Sligo Town, never a call-centre middleman.

How quickly can an emergency plumber reach Sligo Town, Strandhill or Collooney?

Emergency callouts across Sligo Town, Strandhill, Rosses Point, Collooney, Ballysadare and Drumcliffe typically take 15 to 35 minutes.

What is the first thing to do when a pipe bursts in my house?

Turn the brass mains water stopcock clockwise under your kitchen sink, switch off the electric immersion and boiler, and turn the red wheel-head gate valves in your hot press clockwise to stop the attic tank feeding the leak.

How much is an emergency plumber callout in Sligo?

Standard weekday daytime callouts (8am to 6pm) are €90 to €140 including diagnosis and the first hour's fault-finding; evening and Saturday callouts are €135 to €195, and Sunday or night rates are agreed upfront on the phone.

What if the red gate valve in my hot press is seized and water keeps leaking?

Open both the cold and hot bath taps fully so the attic cold storage tank drains safely down the bath waste pipe instead of coming through your ceiling while we are on the way.

Can you fix a split or leaking copper hot water cylinder in the hot press?

Yes. We isolate the cold feed from the attic tank, syphon-drain the leaking copper cylinder safely outside with a hose, cap the feeds so you keep cold mains water at the kitchen tap, and fit a replacement insulated cylinder.
