3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Emergency Roofing)

When will the roofer ring me?

Send the 3-tap form at any time. The roofer rings back 6am to 7pm, seven days a week. If there's a danger to life, such as a wall or chimney about to fall onto a road, ring 112 or 999.

How much does an emergency roof repair cost?

A few slipped slates are €350 to €1,100, a chimney flashing €650 to €2,500 and storm damage €600 to €3,000+. The roofer gives you the price after seeing it, and nothing is agreed until you accept. What a roof repair costs has every job.

Will my insurance pay?

Storm damage is usually covered, wear and tear isn't. Insurers usually pay for temporary repairs, so keep the receipts. Your excess, generally €100 to €300, comes off any claim. The insurance claim guide has the steps.

Should I go up and look myself?

Not in wind or rain, and not without someone holding the ladder. Most of what the roofer needs to know you can see from the ground or the attic.

Will they make it safe on the first visit?

That's the job of the first visit: refix or replace what's off, or cover it, so no more water gets in. The proper repair follows in dry weather if it's needed.

Which areas do you cover?

Louth, Meath, Monaghan, Cavan and north Dublin: Dundalk, Drogheda, Ardee, Navan, Ashbourne, Trim, Kells, Monaghan town, Carrickmacross, Castleblayney, Clones, Cavan town, Virginia, Bailieborough, Cootehill, Swords, Balbriggan, Malahide, Skerries, and the towns and townlands between.
