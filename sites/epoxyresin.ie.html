3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Epoxy Resin)

Can I do it myself with a kit?

For small pieces, yes: trays, coasters, a shelf, a small tabletop. Anything you walk on, cook on or shower against is a different job. The preparation and the cure decide whether it lasts, and a failed pour has to be scraped or ground off. See DIY or hire a pro .

How much does epoxy resin work cost?

A resin floor costs €30 to €130 a m² fitted. A poured epoxy worktop is about €540 to €840 a m², and a resin coat over the worktop you have about €300 to €480 a m². Jointless shower walls run €60 to €120 a m². See what epoxy resin work costs .

Is epoxy resin safe in a kitchen?

Fully cured, it's a hard, inert plastic. Before that it isn't: it needs about a week at room temperature. Ask for the product's food contact suitability in writing. Since 20 January 2025 the EU has banned BPA in food contact varnishes and coatings. See is epoxy resin food safe .

Can I put a hot pan on it?

No. Most table-top epoxies start to soften at 38 to 54°C. A pan off the hob or a kettle leaves a mark. Use a trivet. See heat resistance .

Will it go yellow?

In time, yes, and sunlight is the main cause. UV stabilisers slow it; they don't stop it. Pale and white colours show it first. See yellowing .

Is a resin driveway epoxy?

No. A resin bound driveway is stone mixed with a UV-stable polyurethane resin, so it doesn't yellow outside and water drains through it. See epoxy vs resin bound .
