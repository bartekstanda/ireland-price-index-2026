3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (EV Charger Installation Ireland)

How much does it cost to install a home EV charger in Ireland?

A standard 7 kW home EV charger installation in Ireland typically costs €899 to €1,595 supplied and fitted before the grant, or €599 to €1,295 after the €300 SEAI Home Charger Grant.

How much is the SEAI Home Charger Grant in 2026?

SEAI pays a flat €300 grant towards the purchase and installation of a smart home EV charger at a private residence with off-street parking.

Do I need to own an electric car to claim the €300 SEAI charger grant?

No. Under current SEAI rules you do not need to own or have ordered an electric vehicle to apply for the €300 Home Charger Grant, provided the home has a dedicated off-street parking space.

What happens if the electrician fits the charger before SEAI approves my application?

You lose the €300 grant. SEAI will not pay if installation work starts before the issue date on your official Letter of Offer.

Who is allowed to install a home EV charger in Ireland?

By law and for SEAI grant payment, the installation must be carried out and certified by a Safe Electric Registered Electrical Contractor (REC) who issues a Certificate Number 3.

What is the difference between a tethered and an untethered EV charger?

A tethered charger has a charging cable permanently attached (usually 5 m to 7.5 m Type 2) so you plug straight into the car; an untethered charger has a Type 2 socket so you use the cable from your car boot.
