3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (EV Home Charger)

How long does an electric car take to charge at home?

From empty, about 8h 30m to 12h 30m on a 7 kW charger for the cars on this site. Day to day, only the day's driving: 60 km in a Model Y takes about 1h 35m.

Can I use a 3-pin plug?

Yes, at about 2.3 kW. It suits short daily driving.

Why is my charger 7 kW and not 11?

Most Irish homes have a single-phase supply, and a home charger on single phase gives up to 7.4 kW, which is why people call it a 7 kW charger. Faster 11 or 22 kW charging needs a three-phase supply, which few homes have, and a car whose on-board charger takes it.

Is there a grant?

Up to €300 from SEAI towards a smart charger and its installation, for a home with off-street parking.

What does a home charger cost?

Fitted, a 7 kW smart charger typically costs €1,000 to €1,500 before the grant.

I have no driveway. What then?

The grant needs off-street parking. Charging without a driveway has the options.
