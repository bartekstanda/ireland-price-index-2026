3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (External Insulation Grant)

How much is the external insulation grant?

SEAI pays a fixed external wall insulation grant: €8,000 for a detached house, €6,000 for a semi-detached or end-of-terrace house, €3,500 for a mid-terrace and €3,000 for an apartment. The home must have been built and first lived in before 2011, and every outside wall must be done.

What's left to pay after the grant?

About €17,200 on a typical semi-D, €10,500 on a mid-terrace and €18,500 on a detached house, more on bigger houses.

Is the grant bigger for a bigger house?

No. It's fixed by house type.

Can I get it after a cavity or dry lining grant?

Yes. Since 2 March 2026 external insulation can be a second wall measure after a cavity or internal wall grant.

Can I get a second external grant?

No. After an external wall grant there's no second wall measure.

Does every wall have to be done?

Yes. SEAI needs every outside wall of the home insulated.
