3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Fascia and Soffit Cork)

How much does fascia and soffit replacement cost in Cork?

€800 to €1,400 for a mid-terrace, €1,000 to €1,600 for a semi (front and rear) and €2,000 to €3,500 for a detached house, supplied and fitted. With new gutters, a 3-bed semi is €1,400 to €3,500. The Cork price guide has every job.

How long does it take?

One to two days for a semi, two to three for a detached house. Scaffolding or rot behind the old boards adds time.

Can you just repair one section?

Yes. A fascia section up to 3 m is €150 to €300, a soffit section €120 to €250 and a corner or joint €100 to €200. See repairs .

Is there a grant?

Not for fascia and soffit on its own. With SEAI's external wall insulation grant (€6,000 for a semi, €8,000 for a detached house), the soffits and gutters usually need adjusting anyway, so do them together.

Will it make a mess?

Very little. The old boards and gutters come down in sections and leave in the company's van, and the ground under the eaves is swept at the end of each day. The work is all outside.

Is the work guaranteed?

Ask for the guarantee in writing with the price: the company's on the workmanship, and the board maker's on the uPVC and its colour.
