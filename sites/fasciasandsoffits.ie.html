3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Fascias and Soffits)

How much does fascia and soffit replacement cost in Ireland?

€900 to €1,800 for a mid-terrace, €1,000 to €2,500 for a semi and €2,000 to €4,000+ for a detached house, supplied and fitted. With new gutters as well, most houses come in between €1,000 and €4,500. The price guide has the detail.

How long does it take?

One to two days for most houses. A big detached house, scaffolding or rot behind the old boards adds a day or two. See how long it takes .

Is there a grant for fascia and soffits?

Not on its own. If you're getting external wall insulation with the SEAI grant (€6,000 for a semi, €8,000 for a detached house), the soffits and gutters usually have to be adjusted anyway, so do them together.

Will it make a mess?

Very little. The old boards and gutters come down in sections and leave in the company's van, and the ground under the eaves is swept at the end of each day. You don't need to move out or clear rooms: the work is all outside.

Is the work guaranteed?

Ask for the guarantee in writing with the price: one on the workmanship from the company, and the board maker's guarantee on the uPVC and its colour. Good uPVC boards commonly last 20 to 30 years.

Who comes out to my house?

One fascia and soffit company that covers your county. They look at the roofline, price it in writing and do the work. We never send your details to more than one company.
