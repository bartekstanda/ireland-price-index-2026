3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Fibreglass Roofing)

How much does a fibreglass roof cost in Ireland?

A GRP flat roof on a garage or extension costs €2,800 to €6,500, and €3,800 to €6,500 on a 30 to 40 m² kitchen extension. Laid, GRP runs €75 to €140 a m². The cost guide breaks it down.

Is GRP the same as fibreglass?

Yes. GRP is glass-reinforced polyester: glass fibre mat set in resin and finished with a coloured topcoat. Roofers use both names for the same roof.

How long does a fibreglass roof last?

25 to 35 years when it's laid on a sound deck in dry weather. See how long it lasts .

Can GRP go over my old felt roof?

Sometimes, if the deck underneath is dry and sound: €1,800 to €5,000. See GRP over an existing roof .

What if it rains on the day?

Nothing goes down in the wet: GRP laid on a damp deck fails. A good roofer keeps a sheet on site, stops, covers the roof and comes back on the next dry day.

Will there be a smell?

Yes, while the resin and topcoat cure, for a day or so. Keep windows near the roof shut while it's laid; it goes once the roof has cured.
