3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Flat Roof Replacement)

How much does flat roof replacement cost in Ireland?

A kitchen extension of 25 to 40 m² costs €3,000 to €6,500, a single garage €1,800 to €5,200 and a porch or dormer €1,800 to €2,800. The cost guide breaks it down by size, material and county.

Which is better, EPDM, GRP or felt?

EPDM is one sheet with no seams on most house roofs and needs no flame. GRP is hard and can be walked on. Felt costs least and lasts least. See EPDM vs GRP vs felt .

How long does it take?

One to three days on most house flat roofs, weather allowing. See how long it takes .

Can you just fix the leak?

Often, yes. One fault on a sound roof is a repair: €250 to €900. If the deck is soft or the roof leaks in several places, it's a replacement, and the company will say so.

Will the room below be wet or dusty?

The old covering comes off one section at a time, and only as much as can be covered the same day. The room below is protected and the roof is covered at the end of each day.

Is there a grant?

SEAI's roof insulation rules count flat roofs, so insulating one as a warm roof can be part of a roof insulation grant. There's no grant for the membrane alone. Apply before any work starts.
