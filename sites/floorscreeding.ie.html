3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Floor Screeding)

How much does floor screeding cost per m² in Ireland?

Every floor is priced on its own: the area, the depth, the type of screed, how close the pump can get and what's underneath. Send the floor in and you get a written price per m² for your job.

How much is a bag of floor screed?

A 25 kg bag of sand and cement floor screed is about €11.90 without VAT, €14.64 with it. Polymer-modified is about €12.57.

How much is floor levelling compound?

A 25 kg bag of levelling compound is about €35 with VAT, dropping to €31 by the pallet.

How much screed is in 100 m² at 50 mm?

5 m³, weighing 10.5–11 tonnes at 2,100–2,200 kg/m³. Our arithmetic.

How thick should floor screed be?

Sand and cement: 25 mm bonded, 50 mm unbonded, 65 mm floating in a house. Liquid screeds: from 35 mm floating or 45 mm (cement-based liquid screed).

How much screed goes over underfloor heating pipes?

At least 25 mm of cover for a liquid screed. Sand and cement: 65–75 mm total over heating.
