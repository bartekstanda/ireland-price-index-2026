3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Gastric Balloon Ireland)

How much does a gastric balloon cost in Ireland?

Our private price guide puts a gastric balloon in Ireland at €3,400 to €4,900 paying yourself. A 6-month endoscopic balloon typically sits at €3,400 to €4,000, while a swallowable 4-month capsule programme or a 12-month endoscopic balloon usually sits at €3,900 to €4,900. Check that your written quote includes dietitian support and endoscopic removal where needed.

Do you need an endoscopy or general anaesthetic for a gastric balloon?

No general anaesthetic is needed for either type. A swallowable 4-month balloon is swallowed awake as a capsule with water and confirmed by two quick X-rays, with no camera and no sedation, then passes naturally after about four months. A 6-month or 12-month balloon is placed and later removed through the mouth with a flexible camera under light intravenous sedation as a day case.

How rough is the first week after getting a gastric balloon?

Expect three to five uncomfortable days right after placement. Because the stomach treats the saline-filled sphere as food it cannot digest, the stomach wall squeezes repeatedly, causing cramping, nausea, acid reflux and sometimes vomiting until the muscles adapt. Clinics prescribe anti-sickness tablets, antispasmodics and daily acid-suppressing tablets to get you through that window.

How much weight do people lose with a gastric balloon?

Clinical studies average about 10% to 15% of starting total body weight over four to twelve months when paired with structured dietitian changes. That is meaningful, though lower than keyhole bariatric surgery, where the HSE notes 20% to 40% total weight loss. Because the stomach returns to normal size once the balloon leaves, keeping the weight off depends on the eating habits built while it is in place.

Who cannot have a gastric balloon?

Anyone under 18, anyone who is pregnant or breastfeeding, and anyone who has had previous stomach or bariatric surgery cannot safely have a gastric balloon. A large hiatus hernia, an active stomach ulcer, inflammatory bowel disease or long-term anticoagulant use also normally rules it out.

Can I claim 20% tax relief on a gastric balloon in Ireland?

Revenue allows income tax relief at the standard rate of 20% on qualifying health expenses carried out or advised by a registered medical practitioner. Cosmetic procedures do not qualify, whereas medical treatment for obesity or weight-related health conditions generally fits Revenue's health care rules. Read our 20% tax relief guide for how to claim through Revenue myAccount.
