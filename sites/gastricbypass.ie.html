3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Gastric Bypass Ireland)

How much does gastric bypass surgery cost in Ireland?

Private self-pay gastric bypass surgery in Ireland typically costs €7,500 to €12,600. That hospital package normally covers the consultant surgeon, anaesthetist, 2 to 4 nights in a private hospital and structured dietitian and surgical follow-up, though exact inclusions vary and must be checked in writing. For comparison, a gastric sleeve sits at €6,500 to €10,000 and a non-surgical gastric balloon at €3,400 to €4,900.

Why would a surgeon recommend gastric bypass instead of a gastric sleeve?

Two clinical factors point strongly toward a Roux-en-Y gastric bypass: severe acid reflux (or a hiatus hernia or Barrett's oesophagus), which a sleeve can make worse, and type 2 diabetes, which often responds strongly to bypassing the upper small bowel. Total weight loss also averages slightly higher after a bypass, up to 30% to 35% of total body weight over 12 to 24 months compared with up to 25% to 30% after a sleeve.

What BMI do I need for a gastric bypass in Ireland?

Standard clinical guidelines look for a BMI of 40 or over, or a BMI between 35 and 40 with a weight-related condition such as type 2 diabetes, sleep apnoea or high blood pressure. Lower thresholds can apply for people from South Asian, Chinese, other Asian, Middle Eastern, Black African or African-Caribbean backgrounds. Private health insurers often set stricter BMI thresholds of their own.

How long are you in hospital and off work after a gastric bypass?

Keyhole gastric bypass usually means 2 to 4 nights in a private hospital (1 to 3 days on some public pathways). Most people return to a desk job after 2 to 3 weeks, while jobs involving physical strain or heavy lifting need at least 6 weeks off.

Do you really need vitamins for life after a gastric bypass?

Yes. Because food bypasses the lower stomach and duodenum where iron, calcium and vitamin B12 are mainly absorbed, daily bariatric multivitamins, calcium with vitamin D, iron and regular vitamin B12, backed up by lifelong blood tests, are mandatory rather than optional.

Does private health insurance or Revenue 20% tax relief help with the cost?

Many Irish hospital plans include bariatric surgery codes once clinical and policy criteria are met and approved in writing before surgery, subject to waiting periods of up to 5 years for pre-existing conditions and up to 2 years after a plan upgrade. On fees you pay yourself, qualifying medical care attracts 20% income tax relief through Revenue myAccount.
