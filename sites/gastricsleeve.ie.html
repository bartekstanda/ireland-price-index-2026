3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Gastric Sleeve Ireland)

What is a gastric sleeve?

An operation that removes about 80% of the stomach by keyhole surgery, leaving a narrow tube, so you feel full on much less food. Sleeve gastrectomy explained .

How much does a gastric sleeve cost in Ireland?

About €6,500 to €10,000 paying yourself. A bypass is about €7,500 to €12,600 and a balloon about €3,400 to €4,900. The cost in Ireland .

Who is eligible for a gastric sleeve?

Usually adults with a BMI of 40 or more, or 35 to 40 with a weight-related condition such as type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure or sleep apnoea, for whom surgery is safe and who can commit to lifelong follow-up. The criteria .

Can I get a gastric sleeve on the HSE?

Yes, through a GP referral to an HSE specialist obesity service. There's no hospital charge, but the HSE did 208 operations in 2024 and waits can run to years. The HSE route .

Does health insurance cover it?

Many Irish hospital plans cover listed bariatric operations when you meet the criteria and get pre-approval. A pre-existing condition wait of up to five years can apply on a new policy. Insurance .

Can I claim tax relief?

Yes, usually: relief at 20% on what you pay yourself, claimed through Revenue's myAccount. Tax relief .
