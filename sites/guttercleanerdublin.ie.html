3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Gutter Cleaner Dublin)

How does Gutter Cleaner Dublin work?

Tell us about your house and gutters in four taps. You see our price guide straight away, and if you leave your number we pass it to one gutter cleaning company that covers your part of Dublin.

What VAT rate applies to domestic gutter work in Ireland?

Cleaning, repair and replacement work on a home is charged at the reduced 13.5% VAT rate when labour makes up most of the cost. Ask for a written quote with VAT included.

Why do gutters overflow in heavy rain even after a clean?

If the gutter channel is clear and water still spills over the front edge, either the downpipe outlet or underground gulley trap is blocked, or the gutter brackets have sagged and lost their fall towards the downpipe.

How often should gutters be cleared on a Dublin house?

Once a year in late November or December after the autumn leaves are down, or twice a year (late autumn and spring) if mature trees overhang the roof.

What causes a uPVC gutter joint to drip?

The rubber EPDM seal inside the union bracket perishes, shrinks or gets gritty with roof moss, or the gutter lengths were cut slightly short so thermal movement pulls the joint apart.

Can a single leaking gutter joint be repaired without replacing the whole run?

Yes. If the uPVC lengths are not brittle, fitting a new union bracket or rubber seal and adding a support clip beside the joint stops the drip.
