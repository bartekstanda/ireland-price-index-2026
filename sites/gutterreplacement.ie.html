3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Gutter Replacement)

How much does gutter replacement cost in Ireland?

New uPVC gutters and downpipes on a semi cost €600 to €1,900, fitted. Seamless aluminium is €1,200 to €3,400 and cast iron €2,100 to €7,500. The price guide has every job.

How long does it take?

A day for most houses. A big detached house, scaffolding, cast iron or rotten fascia behind the gutters adds time. See how long it takes .

Is there a grant for new gutters?

Not for an ordinary house. On a protected structure, the Built Heritage Investment Scheme run through the councils can help with repairs to historic rainwater goods such as cast iron. With SEAI's external wall insulation grant, the gutters and downpipes have to come off and go back anyway, so plan them together.

Will it make a mess?

Very little. The old gutters come down in lengths and leave in the company's van, and the ground is swept at the end of the day. All the work is outside.

Is the work guaranteed?

Ask for it in writing with the price: the company's guarantee on the workmanship, and the maker's on the gutters and their colour.

Who comes out to my house?

One gutter company that covers your county. They look at the house, price it in writing and do the work. Your details go to one company only.
