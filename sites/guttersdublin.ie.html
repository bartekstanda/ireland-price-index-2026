3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Gutters Dublin)

How much does it cost to clean gutters on a Dublin house?

Clearing gutters and checking the downpipes on a typical 3-bed terrace or semi in Dublin is priced by house size and access, with detached or three-storey houses costing more.

When can a leaking gutter joint be repaired instead of replacing the whole run?

If a uPVC gutter is still straight and not brittle, replacing the perished rubber seal clip at the leaking union bracket or fitting a new running outlet usually stops the drip without replacing the whole run.

What is the difference between uPVC and continuous aluminium gutters?

uPVC gutters come in 4 m lengths joined with rubber-sealed union clips, while continuous aluminium gutters are roll-formed on site in one piece with no intermediate joints along the run.

Why do Victorian and Georgian houses in Dublin have box gutters?

Two-storey-over-basement and parapet roofs in Dublin 4, Dublin 6, Rathmines and Dún Laoghaire sit behind a brick parapet or between two pitched roofs, so rainwater drains along an internal lead-lined or felt-lined timber trough called a box gutter.

Are cast-iron gutters required on Protected Structures in Dublin?

On Protected Structures and houses in Architectural Conservation Areas across Dublin, councils normally require like-for-like cast-iron repair or heritage cast-iron replacement rather than plastic.

Why do gutters overflow in heavy rain even after they are cleaned?

If clean gutters still spill over the front edge, the brackets may have sagged out of fall toward the outlet, the fascia board may be tilting forward, or a single downpipe is undersized for the roof area.
