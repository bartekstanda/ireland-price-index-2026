3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Gynecomastia Ireland)

How much is gynecomastia surgery in Ireland?

Our price guide puts gynaecomastia surgery at €3,000 to €6,900 in Ireland. Smaller cases under local anaesthetic sit at the lower end, €3,000 to €3,300; gland removal with liposuction under a general anaesthetic in hospital is €6,500 to €6,900; and surgery that also removes skin starts from about €6,500.

Is gynecomastia the same as gynaecomastia?

Yes. Gynecomastia is the American spelling, gynaecomastia the Irish and British one. Both mean enlarged breast tissue in men, and male breast reduction is the operation that treats it.

Does liposuction alone fix gynecomastia?

Only when the fullness is mostly fat. True gynaecomastia is a firm disc of gland behind the nipple, and liposuction can't remove it, so most men need the gland cut out as well.

How long is recovery?

Most men are home the same day, back at a desk job in about one to two weeks, in a compression vest for three to six weeks, and back to chest training after four to six weeks.

Is it covered by the HSE or my health insurance?

Rarely. Public operations are few, and insurers normally treat it as cosmetic unless strict criteria are met and approved in advance.

Will it come back?

The result is usually permanent, because removed gland doesn't grow back. The chest can fill out again if you gain a lot of weight, or if the cause returns: anabolic steroids, some medicines, or a hormone problem.
