3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Gyno Ireland)

How much does gyno surgery cost in Ireland?

Our Irish price guide puts gyno surgery at €3,000 to €6,900 all-in. Compact subareolar gland removal done awake under local anaesthetic sits at €3,000 to €3,300. Combining gland excision with chest liposuction under general anaesthetic in a private hospital runs €6,500 to €6,900, and cases needing loose skin tightened start from about €6,500.

Why did getting lean make my gyno look worse?

Calorie deficits shrink fat cells across your upper chest and abs, stripping away the soft padding around the nipple. True breast gland is dense fibrous tissue that does not burn off as fuel, so the firm disc underneath stands out sharper under thin t-shirts once you drop body fat.

Can heavy bench pressing flatten puffy nipples?

No. Pressing and flyes thicken the pectoralis major muscle deep to the gland. Building a bigger pec shelf pushes an untreated subareolar gland button further forward against the skin rather than shrinking it.

What is the difference between awake local and general anaesthetic gyno removal?

Leaner men with a compact gland button behind the nipple often suit tumescent local anaesthetic in a clinic minor-ops theatre (€3,000 to €3,300), walking out the same morning or afternoon. Broader chests needing wide liposuction feathering across the pec borders usually use general anaesthetic in hospital (€6,500 to €6,900).

How noticeable is the compression vest under work clothes?

Modern post-op vests are thin, matte elastic garments cut like a tight sleeveless base layer. Under an Oxford shirt, quarter-zip, jumper or dark polo they rarely show, and most men return to desk or hybrid work within one to two weeks while wearing one.

When can I bench press and train upper body again?

Easy outdoor walks start in the first couple of days, and light lower-body machines or stationary cycling often resume around week two or three if cleared by your surgeon. Direct chest, shoulder and lat work waits four to six weeks, building back to normal loads at six to eight weeks.
