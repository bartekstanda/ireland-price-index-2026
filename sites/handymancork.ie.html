3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Handyman Cork)

How is a job priced by Handyman Services Cork?

Every job is priced after a free site visit (or from clear photos and measurements for smaller jobs) as one fixed figure in writing before any work starts, with no deposit.

Who comes out to do the work if I accept the quote?

The same person who visits, measures and quotes the job carries out the work from start to finish and takes all offcuts away.

Which areas of Cork do you cover?

Cork city and county, Monday to Saturday from 9:00 to 18:00.

Can you hang new internal doors into older out-of-square door frames?

Yes. Each new door is planed and scribed on site to fit the existing lining, hinge recesses are chiselled flush, and latches are aligned so the door closes with an even margin.

Can you stop stairs from creaking or fix a loose banister handrail?

Yes. Creaking stair treads are secured back to the risers and strings from underneath where accessible or from above, and loose newel posts, spindles and handrails are re-anchored into solid framing.

What is the difference between composite decking and pressure-treated timber decking?

Composite decking boards are made from wood fibres and recycled plastic that do not need yearly staining or oiling, while pressure-treated softwood costs less upfront for the boards and can be stained or oiled to your chosen colour.
