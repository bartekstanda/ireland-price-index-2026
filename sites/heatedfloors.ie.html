3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Heated Floors)

How much does underfloor heating cost in Ireland?

A wet system in a new build or extension is typically €50 to €90 per m² supplied and fitted, about €5,500 to €9,900 for a three-bed house. An electric mat for a 4 m² bathroom is about €240 incl. VAT, before the electrician. A retrofit is priced after a look at the floor.

How much is underfloor heating per m²?

A wet system in a new build costs about €50 to €90 per m² supplied and fitted.

How much do electric underfloor heating mats cost?

About €104 for 1 m², €240 for 4 m² and €488 for 10 m², incl. VAT.

How much does a manifold cost?

About €157 for 2 ports to €378 for 12 ports, incl. VAT.

Is there a grant for underfloor heating?

Not on its own. SEAI's only money for underfloor heating is the central heating grant: up to €2,000 (€1,000 for an apartment) for new radiators or underfloor heating, as part of the heat pump system grant, which totals up to €14,500 for a house.

How much is the SEAI heat pump grant?

Up to €14,500 for a house and €11,500 for an apartment, including up to €2,000 for radiators or underfloor heating.
