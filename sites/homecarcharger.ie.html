3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Home Car Charger)

How much does it cost to charge an EV at home?

Charging at home costs about €6.05 per 100 km on a standard rate in a Tesla Model Y, €2.71 on a night rate and €1.56 in an EV tariff's cheap window. A petrol car using 6 litres per 100 km costs about €11.89.

How much is a full charge?

For a Tesla Model Y, about €10.18 on a night rate.

Is a night rate or an EV tariff better?

An EV tariff's window is cheaper, but shorter, and the rest of the day can cost more. Compare the whole bill.

Do I need a smart meter?

For an EV or time-of-use plan, yes.

Is electric cheaper than petrol?

At home, yes: about €2.71 per 100 km on a night rate in a Model Y, against €11.89 for a petrol car using 6 litres.

Is there a grant for a home charger?

Up to €300 from SEAI, for a smart charger and its installation, with off-street parking.
