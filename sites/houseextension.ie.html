3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (House Extension)

How much does a house extension cost in Ireland?

Building on one level costs €1,500 to €2,200 per m² across Ireland, and building two storeys €2,000 to €2,500 per m² with both floors counted, VAT at 13.5% included. Dublin and the commuter counties sit higher. With design and engineer's fees, a 20 m² ground-floor room comes to €42,000 to €54,000 and a 40 m² two-storey extension with an en-suite to €105,000 to €128,000.

How much is an extension per m²?

Per m² across Ireland, VAT included: one storey €1,500 to €2,200, two storeys €2,000 to €2,500, a side extension €1,800 to €2,400, a wraparound €2,200 to €3,200 and mostly glass €2,800 to €3,500. Every line is higher in Dublin.

How much does a garage conversion cost?

€15,000 to €25,000 at a standard finish, €20,000 to €30,000 mid-range, €30,000 to €45,000 high spec or with an en-suite.

How much does an en-suite cost to add?

€4,000 to €8,000 to fit out with standard fittings, €10,000 to €18,000 mid-range, €20,000 to €40,000 high-end, inside the new room.

Can I extend without planning permission?

Since 27 July 2026 you can build up to 45 m² behind the house without applying for planning permission. That 45 m² includes every extension added since 1964 and any attached garage you convert. The extension can't rise above the house, the house has to keep 25 m² of private open space, ground-floor windows need 1 m or more to the boundary they look onto, and no more than 12 m² of it can be upstairs on a semi or terrace (20 m² on a detached house). Before that date the limit was 40 m².

What changed in July 2026?

On 27 July 2026 the exempt area behind the house rose from 40 m² to 45 m², the distance from an upstairs window to the boundary came down from 11 m to 8 m, and the building control threshold moved to 45 m².
