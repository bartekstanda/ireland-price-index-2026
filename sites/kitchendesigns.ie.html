3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Kitchen Designs Ireland)

How much does a new fitted kitchen cost in Ireland?

A compact or galley fitted kitchen on the same layout costs €4,500 to €10,000 supplied and fitted. Most mid-range family kitchens (12 to 18 units in painted Shaker or handleless J-pull) cost €9,000 to €20,000, with the typical spend between €10,000 and €15,000. High-spec bespoke or in-frame painted kitchens with an island and stone worktops run from €13,500 to €35,000.

How much room do I need around a kitchen island?

Plan for a clear walkway of 1,000mm to 1,200mm on every working side of the island so oven, dishwasher and opposite drawer units can open while someone walks past. With a standard 600mm wall run and a 900mm deep island, your room needs to be at least 3.6m to 3.9m wide.

What is the difference in price between laminate and quartz worktops?

A high-pressure laminate worktop costs €50 to €100 per linear metre (€800 to €2,000 supplied and fitted in 1 day). Laser-templated 20mm or 30mm quartz or Dekton sintered stone costs €2,200 to €4,800 fitted and takes 5 to 10 working days from templating after the base cabinets are levelled.

How long does it take to design and fit a new kitchen?

Once you approve the 3D CAD plan and finishes, cabinet manufacturing takes 3 to 6 weeks (or 4 to 8 weeks for bespoke painted in-frame doors). On-site fitting takes 3 to 5 days for cabinetry and laminate tops, or 2 to 3 weeks altogether if walls, plumbing, electrics, plastering and stone templating are involved.

Do I need planning permission to knock the kitchen-dining wall?

Internal wall removals are exempt from planning permission in Ireland unless the house is a Protected Structure. However, if the wall between the kitchen and dining room is load-bearing, a structural engineer must size an RSJ steel beam and concrete padstones (€2,000 to €4,000 supplied and fitted) so Building Regulations Part A are met and an Opinion on Compliance can be issued when you sell.

What comes included in a fitted kitchen quote?

A standard fitted kitchen quote covers the home survey, 3D CAD drawings, rigid 18mm cabinets, doors, end panels, plinths, soft-close hinges and drawers, worktops and cabinet installation. Always check whether ripping out the old kitchen, skip hire, plumbing, electrical certification and tiling are itemised.
