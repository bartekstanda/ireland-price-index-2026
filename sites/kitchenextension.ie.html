3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Kitchen Extension)

How much does a kitchen extension cost in Ireland?

A single-storey kitchen extension costs €1,500 to €2,200 per m² to build across Ireland and €1,800 to €2,500 in Dublin, including VAT. A 25 m² extension with a new kitchen and the fees comes to €45,000 to €98,000 all in, depending on where you live and the finish.

How much is an extension per m² in Ireland?

Single-storey €1,500 to €2,200 across Ireland and €1,800 to €2,500 in Dublin; side return €1,800 to €2,400; wraparound €2,200 to €3,200; two-storey €2,000 to €2,500; heavily glazed €2,800 to €3,500. All including VAT.

Do I need planning permission for a kitchen extension?

No planning permission is needed for a rear extension of up to 45 m², counting any earlier extensions built since 1964, if it meets the conditions: no higher than the house, at least 25 m² of private open space left, ground-floor windows at least 1 m from the boundary they face, and upstairs no more than 12 m² on a semi or terrace (20 m² detached). The limit rose from 40 m² on 27 July 2026.

What changed in July 2026?

From 27 July 2026 the exempt rear extension went from 40 m² to 45 m², upstairs windows can be 8 m from the boundary instead of 11 m, and the building control threshold moved to 45 m² too.

Do I need a commencement notice?

Not for an extension of up to 45 m² that is exempt from planning. Yes, 14 to 28 days before work, if it's bigger or needed planning permission.

What is BCAR?

Building control certification for extensions over 45 m²: a design certifier and an assigned certifier, or the owner opts out.
