3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Kitchen Redesign)

What is a kitchen redesign?

Changing how the kitchen works and looks: where the sink, cooker and fridge sit, how much worktop and storage you get, and the doors, colour and worktop. It can be as small as a respray or as big as a new layout with a wall taken out.

How does the free Redesign Studio work?

Take or upload a photo of your kitchen, then choose the layout, style, door colour, worktop and finishing touches. Add your first name, mobile and county, and the Studio draws your redesign on your own photo in about 20 seconds. Ask the AI designer for changes and flip between versions. It's free. If you tap Get this kitchen priced, we pass your design to one kitchen fitter who covers your area, who rings you to measure up and quote.

Is the Studio picture what my kitchen will look like?

It's a preview drawn by AI on your own photo, not a design drawing, a survey or a guarantee. The fitter who measures up checks what fits the room, where the plumbing and electrics are, and what it costs.

How much space does a kitchen island need?

1200 mm between the island and the other units on every side you walk, plus the island's own depth. Less than that and a peninsula off the end of a run works better.

How much does a kitchen redesign cost?

From €700 to €1,000 to respray a small kitchen, €1,350 to €5,000 for new doors, drawer fronts and a worktop on your units, and €9,000 to €20,000 for a new mid-range kitchen. Moving the sink, cooker or a wall adds to it. Our full price guide .

How long does it take?

A respray is one to three days. New doors and a laminate worktop, two or three days. A new kitchen is three to five days of fitting, two to three weeks with tiling, electrics and plumbing.
