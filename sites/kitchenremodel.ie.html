3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Kitchen Remodel)

How much does a kitchen remodel cost in Ireland?

€4,500 to €10,000 for a budget or flat-pack kitchen, €9,000 to €20,000 for a mid-range one and €13,500 to €35,000 for high-specification or bespoke, fitted. Keeping the units and changing the doors and worktop is €1,350 to €4,000.

Kitchen remodel or refresh: which do I need?

A refresh keeps the units you have and changes what you see: a respray from €700 for a small kitchen, or new doors and a worktop for €1,350 to €4,000. A remodel replaces the units, and is the one to price if they're damaged or the layout doesn't work. Every refresh option .

Is the Studio picture what my kitchen will look like?

It's a preview drawn by AI on your own photo, so you can see a look before anyone measures. It isn't a design drawing or a promise of the finished kitchen.

How accurate is the estimator?

It shows our price guide for the job you pick. It isn't a quote: a fitter prices your kitchen after measuring it.

How long does a kitchen remodel take?

Fitting takes three to seven days on site. With tiling, plumbing and electrics it's two to three weeks, and from first design to finished, four to eight weeks. Every job, timed .

Will I be without a kitchen?

For the days the old one comes out and the new one goes in: usually a week, a little longer with a stone worktop, which is templated once the units are in. Set up a kettle and a microwave in another room.
