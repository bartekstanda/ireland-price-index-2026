3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Kitchen Renovation Dublin)

How much does a kitchen renovation cost in Dublin?

A typical made-to-measure family kitchen with a quartz worktop costs €15,000 to €25,000 fitted, VAT included. A budget kitchen is €5,000 to €10,000 fully installed, and luxury kitchens start around €25,000. A respray of your existing doors costs €700 to €3,000.

Is a kitchen remodel the same as a kitchen renovation?

Yes. Both mean replacing the units, doors and worktop, and sometimes moving the layout. A refresh or makeover keeps the units and changes only the doors, paint or worktop.

How long does a kitchen renovation take?

Fitting takes 3 to 5 days, and a whole project 2 to 3 weeks, or 4 to 6 weeks for bespoke. A made-to-measure kitchen is fitted about 12 weeks after you sign off the design. A respray takes 1 to 3 days.

Do I need planning permission for a kitchen extension in Dublin?

Not for a rear extension of up to 45 square metres, counting earlier extensions, if it meets the conditions: no higher than the house, and at least 25 square metres of private open space left. The limit rose from 40 to 45 square metres under the 2026 exempted development rules. Dublin's four councils apply the same national rules.

Do Dublin kitchen prices include VAT?

Ask for it on the quote, in writing. The family-kitchen range above includes VAT. Revenue taxes the supply and installation of a fixture at the reduced rate, subject to the two-thirds rule, and supply-only fixtures at the standard rate.

Will the house be a mess?

For about a week. The old kitchen comes out, the new one goes in, and a stone worktop is fitted a week or two after the units. Ask the company how they protect floors and where the skip goes.
