3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Labiaplasty Ireland)

How much does labiaplasty cost in Ireland?

Our price guide puts a labiaplasty at €3,000 to €7,000 in Ireland, all in. Under local anaesthetic in a day clinic it starts from about €3,300. The price normally covers the surgeon, the anaesthetic, the clinic or hospital and your check-ups.

Is it normal for one side of the labia to be longer?

Yes. Labia vary a lot from woman to woman, in length, shape, colour and how much they show. One side is often longer than the other. Inner lips that hang below the outer lips are common and normal.

How long is recovery from labiaplasty?

Most people take about a week off a desk job, up to two weeks for physical work. Swelling and soreness settle over about two weeks, sex and strenuous exercise wait four to six weeks, and the skin takes a couple of months to heal fully.

Is labiaplasty done under general anaesthetic?

It can be done under a local anaesthetic, with or without sedation, or under a general anaesthetic. The operation takes about one to two hours, and most people go home the same day.

Can I get labiaplasty on the HSE or my health insurance?

Irish health insurance plans normally exclude cosmetic surgery, except to correct disfigurement from a condition you were born with, an accident or a disease. Labiaplasty for appearance or comfort is almost never covered. Check your own policy in writing before anything is booked.

What age can you get labiaplasty?

Only adults. Labiaplasty shouldn't be done on anyone under 18 for how the labia look, because the labia keep developing into early adulthood. If you're under 18 and something hurts, itches or worries you, talk to your GP. GPs see young people in confidence.
