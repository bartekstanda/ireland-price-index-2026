3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Laser Eye Correction)

What is the difference between laser eye correction and laser eye surgery?

None: they are two names for the same treatments. Lens-based treatments (implantable lenses and lens replacement) are surgery without a laser.

Am I suitable for laser eye correction?

Usually yes if you are over 18, your prescription has been stable for a year or more and your eyes are healthy. Use the check above, then a full assessment decides.

Which treatment is right for me?

LASIK, LASEK or SMILE for most everyday prescriptions; an implantable lens for high prescriptions or thin corneas; lens replacement from about 50.

How much does laser eye correction cost in Ireland?

Laser treatment costs €800 to €2,600 per eye. Implantable lenses and lens replacement cost €2,900 to €3,900 per eye. Tax relief at 20% applies to all of them.

Does it hurt?

Numbing drops mean you feel pressure rather than pain during treatment. Surface treatment (LASEK and PRK) is sore for a few days afterwards; LASIK and SMILE much less.

Will I need glasses again?

About 1 in 10 people need a top-up. And everyone's close-up focus fades from the mid-40s, so reading glasses usually arrive then even after laser.
