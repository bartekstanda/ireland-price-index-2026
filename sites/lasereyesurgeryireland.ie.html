3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Laser Eye Surgery Ireland)

How much is laser eye surgery in Ireland?

€800 to €2,600 per eye, so €1,600 to €5,200 for both eyes. Most people pay €1,500 to €2,600 an eye.

How much is laser eye surgery for both eyes?

€1,600 to €5,200, or €1,280 to €4,160 after the 20% tax relief.

Is laser eye surgery covered by health insurance?

Not usually. Irish Life Health and Laya healthcare list member discounts of 15% and 10% at partner clinics.

Can I get tax relief on laser eye surgery?

Yes. Revenue treats laser treatment for defective eyesight as health care: you claim 20% back through myAccount.

Is there finance for laser eye surgery?

Many clinics offer monthly payments, some at 0% over 10 or 12 months. Check the APR and the total repayable.

Does the consultation cost anything?

Assessments range from free to €200. Some clinics take a paid one off the treatment price.
