3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Liquid Screed)

What is liquid screed?

A floor screed mixed to flow: pumped in, self-levelling, knocked flat with a dappling bar. Calcium sulphate (anhydrite or hemihydrate) or cement-based.

Anhydrite or cement-based?

Calcium sulphate allows bigger bays and the heating from day 7. Cement-based takes cement adhesives without a barrier primer. Ask for both prices.

Can liquid screed be laid to falls?

A cement-based liquid screed can't. Use sand and cement where a floor falls to a drain.

How thick is liquid screed?

35 mm floating in a house, 45 mm (cement-based liquid screed), and at least 25 mm over heating pipes.

How long does liquid screed take to dry?

About 1 mm a day to 40 mm, then 2 days a mm. 50 mm: about 60 days.

When can the heating go on?

Day 7 on calcium sulphate, day 14 on cement-based liquid screed at no more than 45°C.
