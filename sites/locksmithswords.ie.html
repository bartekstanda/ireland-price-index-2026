3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Locksmith Swords)

How do I check that a locksmith in Swords holds a PSA licence?

Every locksmith working in Ireland must hold an individual or contractor licence from the Private Security Authority (PSA). You can check the public register at psa.gov.ie or ask to see their PSA licence card on arrival.

Can a locksmith open a locked door without drilling the lock?

In most home lockouts where the key is inside or lost and the lock is not broken, a skilled locksmith opens the door non-destructively using picks, a letterbox tool or a bypass slip so you keep your existing lock.

What is lock snapping and which doors in Swords are at risk?

Lock snapping breaks a standard brass euro cylinder in half at the central fixing screw hole to reach the locking cam inside a uPVC, composite or patio door; upgrading to a 3-star anti-snap cylinder stops the attack.

Why does my uPVC door handle lift up hard or fail to lock with the key?

When a uPVC or composite door drops slightly on its hinges or the internal centre gearbox wears out, the hook and roller bolts bind against the frame keeps; re-aligning the hinges or replacing the gearbox before it jams shut saves a much dearer emergency job.

Can you replace just the centre gearbox on a uPVC multipoint door?

Yes. On most uPVC doors the central gearbox unscrews from the full-length metal locking strip, so a locksmith can fit a new gearbox without buying a complete multipoint strip.

Should I change the locks when moving into a new house in Swords?

Yes. Previous owners, tenants, builders or estate agents may still hold spare keys; swapping the euro cylinders or rekeying the locks takes less than an hour and guarantees only your new keys work.
