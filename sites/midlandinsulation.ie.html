3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Midland Insulation)

How much does a combined cavity wall and attic insulation bundle cost after SEAI grants?

For a 3-bed semi-detached home, combining pumped cavity wall insulation and 300mm attic insulation typically costs €1,000 to €1,900 net after the €2,500 combined SEAI grant (€1,200 cavity + €1,300 attic). For a 4-bed detached house or bungalow, the combined grant is €3,200 (€1,700 cavity + €1,500 attic), leaving a typical net cost of €1,300 to €2,600.

What year must my house have been built to qualify for SEAI insulation grants?

Your home must have been built and occupied before 2011 (confirmed by your 11-digit ESB Networks MPRN connection date) to qualify for SEAI Attic, Cavity Wall, Internal Dry-Lining or External Wall Insulation grants.

How do I know if my walls can be pumped with bonded cavity bead?

During a free survey, the technician drills a 12mm test hole in the mortar joint and inspects the cavity with a digital borescope camera to confirm a clear cavity of at least 50mm.

What is Platinum bonded EPS bead and why is it used in cavity walls?

Graphite-enhanced expanded polystyrene (EPS) bead is injected with a water-basedPVA bonding adhesive so the beads lock together inside the wall, preventing slump if an opening is cut later and blocking moisture from crossing the cavity.

How thick should attic insulation be to meet SEAI grant standards?

SEAI requires attic mineral wool or glass wool insulation to be topped up to 300mm total depth (100mm between the ceiling joists and 200mm cross-laid at right angles over the joists) to reach a roof U-value of 0.16 W/m²K.

What else is included in an SEAI-compliant attic insulation job?

Along with 300mm of mineral wool on the attic floor, the installer fits rigid eaves ventilation trays between the rafters so airflow stays open, lags all cold water pipes and the attic storage tank, and insulates and draught-strips the attic hatch.
