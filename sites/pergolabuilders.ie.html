3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Pergola Builders)

How much does a pergola cost in Ireland?

A timber pergola kit is €428 to €733. An aluminium louvred kit is €1,886 to €2,998 for 3 × 3 m to 4 × 3 m, and a motorised roof €3,395 to €7,420. Made to measure and fitted, aluminium starts from €2,500. See the price guide .

How long does it take?

It depends on the base and the roof. A kit going onto a sound, level patio is the quickest job. A made-to-measure aluminium pergola is made to order after the company measures up, so ask for the lead time and the fitting days in writing with the price.

Is there a grant for a pergola?

No. SEAI grants are for energy upgrades, and there's no council grant for garden structures. What you can control is the spec: a timber kit costs a fraction of a motorised roof.

Will it make a mess of the garden?

Not much. On a sound patio the posts bolt down, with no digging. New posts need a pad or footing at each one. Ask the company to take away the packaging and offcuts, and put that in the written price.

What guarantee do I get?

Get it in writing, and separately for the frame, the coating and any motor. Kits in our guide carry 5 years; dealer-fitted aluminium systems can carry 10 years on the structure and longer on the coating.

Who comes out?

One pergola company that covers your county, and only one. They measure up, price it in writing, build it and deal with you directly.
