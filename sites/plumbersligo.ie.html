3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (SOS Property Services Sligo)

Who answers the phone when I ring SOS Property Services Sligo?

Arek Olczak answers the phone himself on 085 708 8979, talks the job through with you, gives you the price before work starts, and carries out the plumbing work himself.

Which towns in County Sligo do you cover?

Based in Sligo town, Arek covers Sligo town, Strandhill, Ballysadare, Collooney, Rosses Point, Grange, Enniscrone, Tubbercurry, Ballymote and Coolaney.

Where is the mains water stopcock usually located in a Sligo house?

Inside the house, the brass mains stopcock is usually under the kitchen sink where the rising main enters; there is also an external shut-off valve inside the boundary meter box at the footpath.

How do I isolate a leaking hot water cylinder in the hot press?

Turn off the electric immersion switch on the wall, then turn the red wheel-head gate valve on the cold feed pipe coming down from the attic tank into the bottom of the cylinder clockwise until it stops.

Why is one radiator cold at the top while warm at the bottom?

Trapped air rises to the top of the radiator panel and stops hot water circulating; bleeding the air out with a radiator key at the top corner pin valve lets the radiator fill with hot water again.

Why is a radiator cold at the bottom or in the middle?

Dark magnetite sludge settles in the bottom waterways of older radiators and slows circulation; flushing the radiator, balancing the lockshield valves or cleaning the magnetic system filter restores heat.
