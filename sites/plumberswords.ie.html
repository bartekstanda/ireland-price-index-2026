3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Plumber Swords)

How much does a plumber cost in Swords?

Up to €150 to call out on a weekday and €50–€130 an hour after that. Out of hours, the call-out is €100–€250. A dripping tap is €40–€150, a blocked drain €80–€250.

How fast will a plumber ring me?

Usually within one working day, and sooner for an emergency: pick Today in the form. We don't promise a time we can't keep.

Is the plumber registered for gas?

Gas work goes only to a Registered Gas Installer (RGI). It's the law in Ireland. Ask to see the RGI ID card, which has a photo and a registration number.

Is the work guaranteed?

Ask the plumber for their guarantee on the work and on any parts before you agree the price. New parts carry the maker's warranty.

Who will come out?

One local plumber who covers Swords. We pass your request to them only, not to a list. They ring you, agree the price, and do the job.

Do I pay you anything?

No. Sending the form is free. You pay the plumber for the job, at the price you agree with them.
