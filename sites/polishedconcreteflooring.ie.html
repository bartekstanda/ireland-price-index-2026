3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Polished Concrete Flooring)

How much do polished concrete floors cost?

Most floors are €90 to €150 a m². Large floors with a standard finish start at €55 to €60 a m²; small floors and high-end finishes start at €120 a m². Rural jobs add €20 to €30 a m². See our price guide .

Can you polish an existing concrete floor?

Often, yes, and then the grinding and polishing is the whole cost. Whether yours polishes well depends on the concrete, what's been stuck to it and the cracks. A test grind shows it. See polishing an existing floor .

How long does it take?

A new slab cures for at least 28 days before the grinding starts. The polishing itself takes days, not weeks, depending on the size and the finish. How long it takes .

How much mess is there?

The machines grind wet or with dust extraction, so ask which. On a new build it's done before the kitchen goes in. In a lived-in house the room has to be empty for the days it takes.

Does it work with underfloor heating?

Very well. The pipes go in the slab, and the concrete holds and releases the heat slowly. Keep the water no hotter than 35°C and run it low all the time. See underfloor heating .

Is it cold or slippery?

It feels cool without heating, which is why most new polished floors in houses go over underfloor heating. Dry, it's as slip resistant as polished marble, granite or porcelain; for an R10 rating it needs a slip resistant coating.
