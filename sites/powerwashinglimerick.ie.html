3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Power Washing Limerick)

How much does power washing cost in Limerick?

A two-car driveway (30–50 m²) costs €120–€180 to power wash in Limerick, a one-car drive €80–€120 and a large drive €180–€250. A 30 m² patio is €90–€180, and soft washing the walls of a two-storey house €400–€800. Re-sanding block paving adds €2–€4 per m².

How long does it take?

A small driveway is usually done in a few hours. A big or very dirty one, with re-sanding, can take most of a day. Most patios are done in a morning.

Will power washing damage my driveway?

Not when the pressure suits the surface. Tarmac and older concrete get a gentler setting, sandstone gets a soft wash, and block paving gets fresh sand in the joints after.

What about the mess?

The slurry is rinsed off the surface and away from doors, and the company sweeps up. Close windows, move the cars and bring in anything you don't want splashed before they arrive.

Do the prices include VAT?

Cleaning at a house is charged at the reduced VAT rate, 13.5%, by a VAT-registered company. Ask for a written price that shows VAT, so you're comparing like with like.

Who comes out?

One vetted local power washing company that covers Limerick. Your number isn't sent to a list. They ring you, ask about the job or look at your photos, and give you a fixed price. Nothing is booked until you say yes.
