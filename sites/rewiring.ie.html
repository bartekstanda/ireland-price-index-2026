3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Rewiring Ireland)

How much does it cost to rewire a house in Ireland?

A full rewire of a 3-bed house in Ireland costs €7,000 to €14,500, and a 4-bed €11,000 to €19,000. A 2-bed is €6,000 to €11,000 and a 1-bed apartment €4,000 to €9,500, with testing and the Safe Electric certificate included. The cost guide breaks it down.

How do I know if my house needs rewiring?

An old fuse box with fuses instead of trip switches, no RCD on the board, black rubber cable, round-pin sockets, scorch marks or lights that flicker are the usual signs. Testing. A registered electrician tests the insulation and earthing of every circuit and writes it up in a Periodic Inspection Report. The age of the house is a clue, not an answer. See signs you need a rewire .

How long does a rewire take?

4 to 10 days on site for a 3-bed house, and 6 to 12 days for a 4-bed. Living in the house adds a few days, because the work goes room by room.

Can I stay in the house during a rewire?

You can, and many people do. Expect dust, lifted floorboards and parts of the house without power for a few hours at a time. It takes longer and costs about 15 to 20% more than an empty house. See living in the house during a rewire .

Do I need a Safe Electric cert after a rewire?

Yes. The law says a Registered Electrical Contractor must give you a completion certificate for almost all electrical work in a home, and a rewire always needs one. Without it, ESB Networks won't connect a new or altered installation. See the Safe Electric cert .

Is there a grant for rewiring?

Not for everyone. The council's Housing Aid for Older People grant pays up to €10,700 towards rewiring for owners aged 66 or over, and the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant covers rewiring as part of doing up a vacant house. See rewiring grants .
