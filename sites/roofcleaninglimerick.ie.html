3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Roof Cleaning Limerick)

Why shouldn't a tile or slate roof be blasted with a high-pressure washer?

High-pressure jet washing strips the protective surface granules off concrete tiles, forces water uphill under the overlaps into the attic felt, and cracks natural or fibre-cement slate. Scraping the moss by hand and treating the roof with a low-pressure biocide cleans it safely.

How does manual roof moss scraping work?

The operative uses shaped profiling blades that match the curve or flat face of your tile to lift the moss cushions off from the ridge down to the eaves, before bagging the moss and clearing the gutters.

What does the biocide treatment do after the moss is scraped off?

A professional quaternary ammonium biocide (such as DDAC or benzalkonium chloride) is sprayed at low pressure to soak into the pores of the tile and kill the microscopic moss spores, lichen and algae roots so they do not grow back straight away.

Does the roof change colour on the same day a biocide is applied?

Scraping removes all the thick green moss cushions on the day, while the biocide works progressively over the following weeks and months as wind and rain wash away the dead lichen and algae film.

Can a natural slate or fibre-cement slate roof in Limerick be cleaned safely?

Yes, by hand scraping and low-pressure biocide application from roof ladders or a mobile tower without walking directly on unsupported slates.

Are the gutters and downpipes cleared as part of a roof clean?

Yes. The downpipes are disconnected or plugged while the moss is scraped off the roof, then the gutter troughs and downpipe shoes are emptied and flushed clean before leaving.
