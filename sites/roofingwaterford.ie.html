3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Roofing Waterford)

When can a leaking roof in Waterford be repaired rather than replaced?

If the timber rafters and breathable membrane or felt underneath are still sound and the leak comes from a few slipped slates, a cracked ridge tile or perished chimney flashing, a targeted repair fixes the leak without needing a full re-roof.

What causes natural slates to slip on older roofs in Waterford?

Over decades the original iron nails holding the slates rust through (known as nail sickness); once several slates start sliding out on different parts of the roof, re-slating with copper nails onto new treated battens is more reliable than endless patch repairs.

How does a roofer fix a single slipped slate without nailing through the slate above?

The roofer slides the replacement slate into the gap and secures its lower edge with a copper tingles strap hooked over the batten below.

Which flat roof material lasts longest on an extension in Waterford?

EPDM one-piece rubber membrane, GRP fibreglass and two-layer SBS torch-on felt all perform well when laid on a dry deck with a proper fall to the gutter, far outlasting older single-layer mineral felt.

Why do chimneys leak where the brickwork meets the roof slates?

Mortar fillets trowelled against a chimney stack crack as the roof timbers move; cutting Code 4 or Code 5 stepped lead flashing into the mortar joints and dressing a lead apron over the slates gives a permanent weatherproof seal.

What is a dry-fix ridge system and why is it used on windy coastal roofs?

A dry-fix ridge secures every ridge tile mechanically with stainless-steel screws and clips over a ventilated roll instead of relying on sand-and-cement mortar, which can crack and blow off in Atlantic gales along Tramore and Dungarvan.
