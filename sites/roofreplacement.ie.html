3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Roof Replacement)

How much does a new roof cost in Ireland?

On a two-storey semi, €8,500 to €15,000 in concrete tile, €9,000 to €17,000 in fibre cement slate and €16,000 to €24,000+ in natural slate. A detached house is €11,500 to €32,000+. The cost guide breaks it down by house, material and county.

Why do roof quotes vary so much?

Because they often price different jobs. One includes new battens, a breathable membrane, lead flashings, scaffolding, the skip and VAT; another leaves half of it out. The company we pass you to puts every item in writing, so you can see what you're paying for.

How long does a new roof take?

Three to ten working days on most houses, weather allowing, plus a day either side for the scaffolding. The roof is made weather-tight every evening. See how long it takes .

Is there a grant for a new roof?

Not from SEAI for the roof itself. The council's Housing Aid for Older People grant can pay towards repairing or replacing a roof, and the Vacant Property Refurbishment Grant covers roof work on a vacant house. See grants and VAT .

Do I need planning permission?

Not usually to re-roof a house in the same or a similar material. A protected structure, an architectural conservation area or a change of look is a question for the council first. See planning permission .

Will it make a mess of the house and garden?

The old roof comes off into a skip, one section at a time, with sheeting on the scaffold. Expect dust in the attic, so clear it first if you can. Gutters are cleared and the garden is swept before the scaffolding comes down.
