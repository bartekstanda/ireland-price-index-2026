3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Screeding.ie)

How much is screeding per m² in Ireland?

Screeding is priced per m² from your drawings: the area, the depth, the screed, access, the programme and what's included. Send the job and you get a written rate.

How is a screeding quote worked out?

Area, depth and volume, screed type, labour and output, joints and preparation, access, the programme and what's done after the pour.

What does a bag of screed cost?

A 25 kg bag of sand and cement screed is about €11.90 without VAT, €14.64 with it.

How thick is a floating screed?

Sand and cement: 65 mm in housing, 75 mm elsewhere. Calcium sulphate: 35 mm house, 40 mm commercial.

What does CT-C20-F4 mean?

A cementitious screed of compressive class C20 and flexural class F4 under I.S. EN 13813.

What are SR1, SR2 and SR3?

Surface regularity classes named in BS 8204: 3, 5 and 10 mm under a 2 m straightedge.
