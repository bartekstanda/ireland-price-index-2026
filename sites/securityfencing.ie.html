3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Security Fencing)

How much does security fencing cost in Ireland?

Mesh fencing starts from €25 a metre supplied, and a 2.4 m palisade kit is €330 a 2.75 m section. Vehicle gates are €1,184–€3,990. The fitted price depends on the length, the ground, the corners and the gates, so it's priced after a look at the site.

How long does it take to put up?

Ordering often takes longer than fitting. Once the materials are on site, a straight run on good ground goes up quickly; rock, slopes and lots of corners take longer. The contractor gives you dates when they price it.

Is there a grant for fencing?

Not for homes or most businesses. If you're a club, school or farm already in a funding scheme, ask that scheme whether fencing qualifies before you start, because work done first can rule you out.

Do I need planning permission for palisade fencing at home?

Usually, yes. The planning exemption for walls and fences around a house covers capped walls, railings and wooden fences, but not a metal palisade or other security fence. Around a house, palisade needs permission at any height.

Palisade or mesh?

Palisade is the heavier deterrent for yards, depots and industrial sites. Mesh keeps the site visible to cameras and passers-by and suits schools, offices and frontages. Both are hard to climb.

What about the mess?

Post holes are dug or drilled and set in concrete, and the old fence comes out if it's going. The contractor takes away the offcuts and the old fence if that's agreed in the price.
