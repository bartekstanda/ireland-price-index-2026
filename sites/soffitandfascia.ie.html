3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Soffit and Fascia)

How much does soffit and fascia cost?

In uPVC, supplied and fitted: a mid-terrace €900 to €1,800, a semi €1,000 to €2,500, a detached house €2,000 to €4,000+. By the metre, €45 to €98. See cost by house size .

What's the difference between soffit and fascia?

The fascia is the upright board along the roof edge that the gutter is fixed to. The soffit is the flat board underneath, closing the gap back to the wall.

Can new fascia go over the old?

If the old timber is sound, a thin capping board can go over it. If it's soft, it has to come off. See rotten fascia .

My boards are green. Do I need new ones?

Usually not: it's algae, and it washes off uPVC. See cleaning soffit and fascia .

Could my soffit contain asbestos?

Flat grey or cream sheet boards on an older house can be fibre cement, which can contain asbestos. Don't drill or break them. See asbestos soffits .

Is VAT included?

Work on a house is at the reduced rate, 13.5%. Ask for a written price that includes it.
