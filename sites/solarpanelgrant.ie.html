3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Solar Panel Grant)

Who can get it?

Owners of homes built and occupied before 2025, with an MPRN. SEAI can't have supported solar PV at that address before, a BER assessor rates the home after the panels go in, and you must use an SEAI-registered solar company and have the grant offer before work starts.

Do I need a BER?

After the panels are fitted, an SEAI-registered BER assessor does a post-works BER. It's needed to claim the grant, and there's no set rating to reach. A battery-only grant, where the home has no panels, doesn't need one.

Can work start before the offer?

No. Works must not start before you get SEAI's grant offer. Sign with an installer, apply, wait for the offer, then the panels go up. Start first and you lose the grant.

How long does it take?

Allow two to three months from signing. The grant offer comes first and is valid for 8 months. The installer's application to ESB Networks takes at least 4 weeks (20 working days). SEAI asks you to allow up to 6 weeks for payment after the paperwork, longer if it inspects.

Is there much mess or disruption?

Very little. The panels go on from the outside, the inverter goes in the attic, a garage or a utility room, and the power is off for a short while when it's wired into the fuse board.

What is the maximum SEAI solar panel grant in 2026?

Under the standard Better Energy Homes scheme, the maximum grant is €1,800 for solar panels (at 4kWp or above) plus €600 for a 5kWh+ battery, totalling €2,400.
