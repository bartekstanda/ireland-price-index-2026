3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Solar Panels Kerry)

How much is the SEAI solar panel grant for a home in Kerry?

SEAI pays €700 per kWp for the first 2kWp and €200 per extra kWp up to 4kWp, capped at €1,800 for solar panels.

Can I apply for the SEAI solar grant after the panels are fitted?

No. You must apply to SEAI and have your formal letter of offer in hand before any installation work starts on your roof.

What VAT rate applies to home solar panels in Ireland?

Solar panels supplied and installed on private homes qualify for a 0% VAT rate.

Does my house age matter for the SEAI Solar PV grant?

Yes. To qualify for the SEAI Solar Electricity Grant, the home must have been built and occupied before 2021 and must not have received an SEAI solar PV grant previously.

What is the ESB Networks NC6 form?

The NC6 microgeneration notification form is submitted to ESB Networks by your registered installer at least 20 working days before the system is commissioned so your smart meter can record exported power.

How does the Clean Export Guarantee (CEG) pay for exported electricity?

Your electricity supplier pays a per-kWh microgeneration export tariff for surplus solar power sent to the grid, and the first €400 a year of microgeneration export income is exempt from income tax.
