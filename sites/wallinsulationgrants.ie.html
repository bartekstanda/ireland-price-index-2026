3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Wall Insulation Grants)

How much is the SEAI wall insulation grant?

It depends on the job and the home. For a semi-D: €1,300 for cavity wall insulation, €3,500 for internal (dry lining) and €6,000 for external. For a detached house: €1,800, €4,500 and €8,000.

What does wall insulation cost after the grant?

Typically €300 to €600 for cavity wall, €4,100 to €7,800 for internal (dry lining) and €8,300 to €18,500 for external insulation, depending on the home. See the cost guide .

How long does it take?

A cavity fill is usually done in a day. External insulation takes about two weeks on site for an average semi-D, weather permitting. Dry lining goes room by room, so ask the contractor how many days your rooms need.

Is there much mess?

A cavity fill means small holes drilled outside and filled after; nothing happens inside. External insulation means scaffold for a few weeks, and nothing inside either. Dry lining is the messy one: each room is cleared, and sockets, radiators and skirting come off and go back.

What guarantee do I get?

Ask for it in writing before you sign: the product's certificate, the warranty, and who stands over the work afterwards. The contractor fills in SEAI's paperwork for your claim.

Which wall insulation grant can I get?

The one that fits how your walls are built. A cavity wall that's empty gets the cavity grant. A solid or hollow block wall needs internal or external insulation, and you choose which. See which wall insulation .
