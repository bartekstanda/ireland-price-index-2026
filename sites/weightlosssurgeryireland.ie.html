3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Weight Loss Surgery Ireland)

How much does weight loss surgery cost in Ireland?

Private keyhole bariatric surgery in Ireland costs €6,500 to €12,600: a gastric sleeve is €6,500 to €10,000 and a gastric bypass is €7,500 to €12,600. A non-surgical gastric balloon costs €3,400 to €4,900. Qualifying out-of-pocket surgical fees can claim 20% income tax relief through Revenue.

What BMI do you need for weight loss surgery in Ireland?

Standard HSE and Irish clinical guidelines look for a BMI of 40 or over, or a BMI between 35 and 40 with a weight-related condition such as type 2 diabetes, obstructive sleep apnoea or high blood pressure. Lower thresholds may be considered for South Asian, Chinese, Middle Eastern and Black backgrounds.

Should I choose a gastric sleeve or a gastric bypass?

Both are keyhole operations under general anaesthetic. Surgeons often lean toward Roux-en-Y gastric bypass when you have regular acid reflux or type 2 diabetes, and toward sleeve gastrectomy when you want a single-organ operation without rerouting the small bowel. Only a surgeon who examines you can advise.

Can you get weight loss surgery on the HSE?

Yes, through a GP referral into the HSE Obesity Model of Care, with no adult public hospital in-patient charge. Capacity is small: 208 public bariatric operations were carried out in 2024 across Ireland, and waiting lists can run up to four or five years.

Does private health insurance cover weight loss surgery in Ireland?

Some higher hospital plans cover listed bariatric surgery codes when strict clinical and policy criteria are approved in writing before admission. Under Health Insurance Authority rules, new members can face up to a five-year waiting period for pre-existing conditions.

Can I claim 20% tax back on weight loss surgery?

Revenue gives income tax relief at the standard rate of 20% on qualifying out-of-pocket health expenses under Section 469, excluding anything refunded by health insurance or a public body. Claims are made online through Revenue myAccount for PAYE workers or on Form 11 for self-assessed taxpayers.
