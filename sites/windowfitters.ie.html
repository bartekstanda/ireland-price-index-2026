3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Window Fitters)

How much do new windows cost?

A white uPVC window is €300 to €900 supplied and fitted. A 3-bed semi with 10 to 12 windows is €4,500 to €10,000 in uPVC, €9,000 to €17,000 in aluminium. SEAI's median for grant-aided window jobs on a semi is €7,110. See the price guide .

How long does it take?

The windows are made to measure after the survey, which takes 3 to 4 weeks. Fitting is 1 to 2 days on a typical 3-bed semi and 2 to 3 days on a detached house. See how long it takes .

Is there a grant?

Yes. SEAI pays €4,000 for a detached house, €3,000 for a semi, €1,800 for a mid-terrace and €1,500 for an apartment, plus €800 a door for up to two, for homes built and lived in before 2011. Don't start before the offer. See grants and VAT .

Will it make a mess?

Less than people think. One window comes out at a time, so each opening is only open for a short spell and is closed up before the crew leaves. Expect some dust and making good at the reveals; ask for removal of the old frames and the clean-up in the written price.

What guarantee do I get?

Get it in writing: on the frames, the sealed glass units and the fitting, for how long, and from whom. A guarantee on the windows from the maker is worth less if no one stands over the fitting.

Who comes out?

One window company that covers your county, and only one. They survey every window, price it in writing, fit it and deal with you directly.
