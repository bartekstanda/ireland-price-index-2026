3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Window Grants)

Do I have to replace every window?

No. But the windows grant is once per property.

Are roof windows included?

Yes. SEAI includes roof windows and skylights.

What U-value do new windows need?

1.4 W/m²K or better.

Can I get the door grant on its own?

Yes. Windows only, doors only, or both.

Do I need a BER after the work?

Yes. A post-works BER is required, and it is grant-aided.

Can I start before I'm approved?

No. There's no backdating, apart from work ordered or started from 27 January 2026.
