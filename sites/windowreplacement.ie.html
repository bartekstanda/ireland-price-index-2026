3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Window Replacement Ireland)

How much does it cost to replace all windows in a 3-bed semi-detached house in Ireland?

Supplying and fitting 8 to 10 A-rated uPVC windows in a typical 3-bed semi-detached house in Ireland costs €4,500 to €7,800 in smooth white, €5,400 to €9,200 in dual-colour foiled uPVC (such as Anthracite Grey outside and white inside), or €10,500 to €16,800 in triple-glazed Scandinavian aluclad, including removal of old frames, reveal plastering and 13.5% VAT.

Is triple glazing worth the extra cost over double glazing in Ireland?

Yes. Upgrading a full house from A-rated double glazing (1.2 W/m²K) to 40mm–44mm argon triple glazing (0.8 W/m²K) typically adds just 12% to 15% (€600 to €1,100 on a 3-bed semi-detached house), raises internal glass surface temperature to ~18.4°C in winter, stops morning condensation and cuts street noise.

Can I get the SEAI grant for replacing my windows and doors?

Under SEAI rules, the Windows Grant (€1,500 apartment, €3,000 mid-terrace, €3,500 semi-detached, €4,000 detached) and Doors Grant (€800 per door for up to 2 doors) are available for pre-2011 homes achieving Uw ≤ 1.4 W/m²K as part of a whole-house upgrade reaching at least a B2 BER and Heat Loss Indicator (HLI) ≤ 2.0. If your home only needs windows and doors, booking directly with an independent installer often costs less out of pocket than a One Stop Shop package.

How long does a full-house window replacement take?

Once your windows are laser-measured at survey, custom factory manufacture takes 3 to 6 weeks. On-site installation in a lived-in 3-bed semi-detached house takes 2 days (or 3 days for a large 4–5 bed detached house), fitting room by room so your home is never left open overnight.

What Building Regulations apply to replacement windows in Ireland?

Replacement windows must meet Part L (whole-window Uw ≤ 1.4 W/m²K), Part B Fire Safety (every upstairs bedroom requires an unobstructed side-hung fire-escape opening of at least 0.33 m², minimum 450mm × 450mm), Part K/M safety toughened glass in doors and low-level panes below 800mm from the floor, and Part F background ventilation.

How many days does it take to replace all the windows in a 3-bed semi-detached house?

A two- or three-person fitting crew replaces 8 to 10 windows and a front door in a lived-in 3-bed semi-detached house in 2 working days — removing and fitting room by room so no opening is ever left unsealed overnight.
