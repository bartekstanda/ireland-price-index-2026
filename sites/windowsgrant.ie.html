3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Windows Grant)

Why does the windows grant need insulation first?

SEAI's windows and doors grant needs the home to reach a Good or Very Good Heat Loss Indicator (HLI) on the BER Advisory Report after the upgrade. SEAI says an HLI below 2.3 meets it. Attic and wall insulation rated Good or Very Good on your report means you meet the standard.

What is the heat loss indicator?

The heat a home loses for each degree between inside and out, per square metre of floor. SEAI says below 2.3 meets the windows grant rule.

How much is the windows grant?

€4,000 detached, €3,000 semi-D or end of terrace, €1,800 mid-terrace, €1,500 apartment, plus €800 a door for up to two.

Which windows count?

Replacing existing single glazed or older double glazed windows. SEAI includes roof windows and skylights.

Do I need a BER?

A post-works BER for every claim. A pre-works BER is strongly advised if the home has only one type of insulation or neither.

Can I apply for insulation and windows together?

Yes, in one application. The insulation goes in before the windows.
