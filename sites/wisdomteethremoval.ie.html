3. Direct-Answer Consumer FAQs (Wisdom Teeth Removal)

How much does wisdom teeth removal cost in Ireland?

Irish dental practices publish €150 to €270 a tooth for a simple wisdom tooth extraction and €250 to €420 for a surgical removal. A panoramic x-ray adds €50 to €83, and IV sedation €200 to €500. A general anaesthetic is done in hospital and priced at a consultation. A surgical extraction of an impacted wisdom tooth gets 20% tax relief, and medical card holders are covered for extractions at a dentist in the scheme.

Does it hurt?

The removal itself is numbed: you'll usually have a local anaesthetic injection around the tooth, or a sedative or general anaesthetic if that's the plan. Afterwards there's some pain and swelling, worse after a surgical removal. Use the painkillers your dentist recommends.

How long does it take?

The removal itself takes from a few minutes to 20 minutes or longer.

How long is the recovery?

It can take up to 2 weeks to fully recover. Swelling and bruising can last 7 to 10 days, and your dentist may suggest 1 to 2 days off work. Eat soft food for a few days, and don't rinse, spit or have hot drinks for the first 24 hours.

What can I eat afterwards?

Soft or liquid food for a few days, chewing on the other side. Nothing hot for the first 24 hours, and no alcohol.

What is dry socket?

Dry socket is when the blood clot doesn't form in the socket, or comes away. It can happen 3 to 5 days after the removal: an intense, throbbing ache in the gum or jaw, a bad smell or taste, and sometimes bone you can see. Go back to your dentist: they clean the socket and put in a medicated dressing.
